In some communities in Japan, there is a culture of publishing an article every day from December 1 to 25, just like opening the windows of an Advent calendar. Usually, multiple people take turns writing the articles, but sometimes one person writes all of them. This is called a solo Advent calendar.

People call it "torture" or ask, "Why not do it with others?" Solo Advent calendars don't have the best reputation, but I'm going to give it a try this year, in both Japanese and English. The theme of the series is "**My favorite producers and labels in the Japanese dance music scene**."

## Who This Is For

People who are into dance music (e.g., house or dubstep) but don't know much about Japanese dance music.

## Bonus: Why I'm Doing This

Here's why I'm writing this series:

- To practice writing in English.
- To get Japanese producers and labels the attention they deserve. They're just as awesome as anyone else in the world.

Of course, some Japanese producers and labels are already well known outside Japan, and some genres born in Japan, like kawaii future bass, have gone viral around the world. Still, I sometimes feel the scene deserves even more attention. I hope this series helps you discover how great Japanese music is.
